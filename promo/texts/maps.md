Animated World Inflation Map (1960–2024) – Consumer Price Changes (%)

Watch how global inflation has evolved from 1960 to 2024 in this animated map. The video shows annual changes in consumer prices (%), measured by the Consumer Price Index (CPI) in constant 2015 US dollars.

The data comes from the International Monetary Fund (IMF) International Financial Statistics database and is published under a CC BY-4.0 license. This animation highlights trends in global price levels and helps visualize how the cost of goods and services has changed for households worldwide over more than six decades.

🔗 Explore the interactive map here: https://exploring-data.com/map/world/inflation-consumer-prices-annual/

Animated Map of CO₂ Emissions per Capita (1970–2024)

Watch how CO₂ emissions per person have changed across the world over more than five decades in this animated map.

🔗 Explore the interactive map: https://exploring-data.com/map/world/co2-emissions/

This video visualizes annual CO₂ emissions per capita (in metric tons) for every country from 1970 to 2024, using data from the Carbon Dioxide Information Analysis Center (CDIAC) and Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL). The animation makes it easy to see trends, spikes, and shifts in global carbon emissions over time.
